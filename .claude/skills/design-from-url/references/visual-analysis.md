# Visual analysis protocol

Token extraction alone is insufficient. Analyze the page as composition.

## Focal hierarchy

For each important section identify likely reading order and what causes it: scale, contrast, spacing, placement, motion, imagery, surface lift, or color.

## Layout grammar

Determine repeated structures such as centered editorial stacks, split heroes, asymmetric grids, card matrices, open canvas sections, full-width product screenshots, alternating text/media, or dense app-like panels.

Do not universalize one-off compositions.

## Whitespace

Characterize it as expansive, compact, surface-driven, section-driven, grid-driven, intentionally dense, or mixed. Measurements help; the rule matters more than one margin.

## Media

Determine what carries the brand: product UI, photography, illustration, 3D, diagrams, or typography. Document crop/aspect/container behavior.

## Depth

Identify the actual engine: shadows, tonal surfaces, borders/hairlines, blur/glass, gradients, overlap, scale, or near-flat hierarchy.

## Repetition

Repeated visual patterns matter more than unique decoration.

Avoid generic language. Replace "clean and modern" with operational descriptions that another agent can implement.
